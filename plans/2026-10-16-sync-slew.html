Goal

Stop the catch-up spiral for viewers on slow or jittery connections. Today a viewer 0.3 s behind is pushed to play faster, up to 1.5x, which pulls 1.5x the bitrate they already could not sustain, so the fix for a stall causes the next stall. After this plan: the room itself slows to 0.97x while anyone is meaningfully behind (on by default), a lagging viewer slews back at no more than 1.05x and only when it has buffer to spare, nobody is ever seeked forward, and a viewer who wants to jump to the room reloads the page. All rates stay inside the band humans do not notice.

Decisions already taken on #8: room slow-down on by default; laggards only slew; reload is the jump; #7 (sync-aware quality cap, ladder bitrates) comes after this and is out of scope here.

Findings

  • The server already carries a room rate and never uses it. room-for sets :rate 1.0 (src/clj/goob_toob/services/sync.clj:20), state-msg broadcasts it (sync.clj:24-26), expected-pos honours it (src/cljc/goob_toob/sync_core.cljc:8-14). Nothing ever writes another value.
  • The server knows every viewer's lag and stall flag from status! (sync.clj:67-71, fed every 5 s by src/cljs/goob_toob/watch.cljs:280-283) and uses it only to paint the peer list.
  • The client policy is stateless and absolute: policy d (sync_core.cljc:29-41) has no memory, so a viewer hovering at the 0.3 s edge flips in and out of :catch-up every 500 ms tick (watch.cljs:284); and :none forces playbackRate 1.0 (watch.cljs:63-64), which would fight any room rate other than 1.0 (expected advances at 0.97, the element plays 1.0, the viewer drifts ahead 0.03 s/s, nudges to 0.95 after ten seconds, repeat).
  • catch-rate is 1 + 0.1|d| capped at 1.5 (sync_core.cljc:43-48): 0.3 s behind already plays 1.03x forever; 5 s behind plays 1.5x, plainly audible and 50 % more bandwidth. settle-threshold (sync_core.cljc:51) is defined and read nowhere.
  • Behind by 30 s or more hard-seeks (sync_core.cljc:39). For a viewer whose problem is bandwidth, a forward seek lands in an empty buffer: a fresh stall.
  • Peer lag never updates after join. broadcast-peers runs only from the join and leave paths (src/clj/goob_toob/ws.clj:60,72); beat-msg carries no peers (sync.clj:28). The host sees join-time lag until someone leaves.
  • Peer lag sign is inverted on display. status! stores expected − pos (positive when behind, sync.clj:69); ui/fmt-lag prints behind for negative values (src/cljs/goob_toob/ui.cljs:62-66). A viewer 8 s behind shows as "8s ahead".
  • Playback rate is the real mechanism, not timestamp jumps. The client sets video.playbackRate directly (watch.cljs:65-66); dash.js's own setPlaybackRate is the same assignment (node_modules/dashjs/dist/dash.all.debug.js:17506-17508) and its live catch-up feature is built on it. preservesPitch is already set at player create (watch.cljs:155-156).
  • Prior art, from the #8 thread: NTP slews under 128 ms offset and steps above it (our :catch-up / :seek); broadcast time compression runs 2-5 % fast unnoticed and the tempo JND is about 5 %, so 1.05x is the invisible ceiling; Syncplay slows the ahead clients to 0.95x once someone is >1.5 s behind, which costs the laggard no bandwidth and adds to the closing speed. With the room at 0.97 and the laggard at 1.05 the gap closes at 0.08 s/s: 10 s heals in about two minutes with nobody past 5 %.

Plan

1 · sync_core.cljc: tuning map, stateful policy, room-rate (pure)

Replace policy, catch-rate, nudge-slow-rate and settle-threshold with the following. Everything is data in, data out; no platform calls. 

(def tuning
  {:dead-band       1.0    ; behind by less than this: do nothing
   :settle          0.15   ; hysteresis exit for catch-up and nudge
   :nudge-enter     0.5    ; ahead by more than this: slow to :nudge-rate
   :seek-ahead      1.5    ; ahead by more than this: seek back (buffer has it)
   :catch-rate      1.05   ; absolute playbackRate while catching up
   :nudge-rate      0.95   ; absolute playbackRate while ahead
   :runway          8.0    ; seconds of buffered media required to catch up
   :room-slow-rate  0.97
   :room-slow-enter 3.0    ; someone this far behind: room slows
   :room-slow-exit  1.0    ; everyone within this: room back to 1.0
   :status-fresh-ms 15000  ; older status reports do not count
   :gone-after-s    60.0}) ; this far behind is "reload me", not "wait for me"

(defn policy
  "prev is the mode from the last tick, d is local - expected in seconds
  (negative = behind), runway is buffered seconds ahead of currentTime.
  Returns :none, :catch-up, :nudge-slow or :seek-back. Never seeks forward."
  [prev d runway]
  (let [{:keys [dead-band settle nudge-enter seek-ahead] runway-min :runway} tuning]
    (cond
      (> d seek-ahead)                                        :seek-back
      (or (> d nudge-enter) (and (= prev :nudge-slow) (> d settle))) :nudge-slow
      (and (>= runway runway-min)
           (or (< d (- dead-band))
               (and (= prev :catch-up) (< d (- settle)))))    :catch-up
      :else                                                   :none)))

(defn rate-for [mode room-rate]
  (case mode
    :catch-up   (:catch-rate tuning)
    :nudge-slow (:nudge-rate tuning)
    room-rate))

(defn room-rate
  "prev is the room's current rate. clients is a seq of
  {:nick s :drift d :age-ms n} where drift is pos - expected (negative =
  behind) and age-ms is how old the report is. Returns {:rate r :for nick}
  with :for the worst eligible laggard while slowed, else nil."
  [prev clients]
  (let [{:keys [room-slow-rate room-slow-enter room-slow-exit
                status-fresh-ms gone-after-s]} tuning
        eligible (filter #(and (<= (:age-ms %) status-fresh-ms)
                               (<= (- (:drift %)) gone-after-s))
                         clients)
        worst (apply max-key #(- (:drift %)) {:drift 0.0 :nick nil} eligible)
        behind (- (:drift worst))]
    (cond
      (>= behind room-slow-enter) {:rate room-slow-rate :for (:nick worst)}
      (<= behind room-slow-exit)  {:rate 1.0 :for nil}
      :else {:rate prev :for (when (= prev room-slow-rate) (:nick worst))})))

(defn reanchor
  "Change a playing room's rate without moving its position."
  [room now rate]
  (assoc room :pos (expected-pos (assoc room :now now)) :t0 now :rate rate))

Notes for the implementer: max-key with the (- drift) key picks the most-behind client; the seed map makes the empty case return behind = 0. Delete catch-rate, nudge-slow-rate and settle-threshold (their values live in tuning now) and fix the two callers in watch.cljs. drift and expected-pos are unchanged. Keep the ns docstring; add no comments.

2 · sync.clj: lag sign, status age, beat-time rate control, peers on the beat

  • status!: store :drift (- pos expected) and :at (mono-ms) instead of :lag. Positive now means ahead, matching sc/drift. peers-list emits :lag (:drift c) so the wire name and ui/fmt-lag stay as they are and the display sign comes out right.
  • host-cmd!: every op (play, pause, seek) also sets :rate 1.0. The lag data is stale after a host action; the beat re-evaluates within 2 s.

  • New tick-room!: 

    (defn- client-drifts [room now]
  (for [[_ c] (:clients room)]
    {:nick (:nick c) :drift (or (:drift c) 0.0) :age-ms (- now (or (:at c) 0))}))

(defn- tick-room! [room-id]
  (let [now (mono-ms)
        room (get @rooms* room-id)
        {:keys [rate for]} (sc/room-rate (:rate room) (client-drifts room now))]
    (when (and (:playing? room) (not= rate (:rate room)))
      (swap! rooms* update room-id sc/reanchor now rate)
      (println "room" room-id "rate" rate (if for (str "for " for) ""))
      (broadcast room-id (assoc (state-msg (get @rooms* room-id)) :op "rate")))
    (let [room (get @rooms* room-id)]
      (broadcast room-id (assoc (beat-msg room)
                                :slow-for (when (= (:rate room) (:room-slow-rate sc/tuning)) for)
                                :peers (peers-list room))))))

    start-beat-loop! calls tick-room! for each room with clients instead of broadcasting beat-msg directly. A paused room never changes rate (the playing? guard) but still beats.

  • broadcast-peers stays for join/leave (immediate list change).

3 · watch.cljs: room-relative rate, policy memory, runway, status cadence

  • st gains "mode" (init :none).

  • New buffer-ahead: 

    (defn- buffer-ahead [video]
  (let [b (.-buffered video) t (.-currentTime video)]
    (loop [i 0]
      (cond (>= i (.-length b)) 0.0
            (and (<= (.start b i) t) (<= t (.end b i))) (- (.end b i) t)
            :else (recur (inc i))))))

  • apply-policy! becomes: 

    (defn- apply-policy! [st video]
  (when (and (.-started st) (.-room st) (not (.-buffering st)))
    (let [room (.-room st)
          expected (expected-now st room)
          d (sc/drift (.-currentTime video) expected)
          mode (sc/policy (.-mode st) d (buffer-ahead video))
          rate (sc/rate-for mode (:rate room))]
      (set! (.-mode st) mode)
      (when (= mode :seek-back) (set! (.-currentTime video) expected))
      (when (> (sc/abs (- (.-playbackRate video) rate)) 0.001)
        (set! (.-playbackRate video) rate))
      (set! (.-textContent lag-hint)
            (if (< d -10.0)
              (str "you're " (int (- d)) "s behind the room — reload to jump to it")
              "")))))

    lag-hint is a new :span.muted under the video in shell, passed via refs. The hint is the only UI for being far behind; there is no button, by decision.

  • apply-state-msg!: "pause" keeps setting playbackRate 1.0; "play" and "seek" unchanged; "rate" needs no branch (the room map is already replaced at the top of the fn, the next tick applies it).
  • "beat" handler: additionally render-peers! from (:peers msg) and set a slow-note span in the host row to "room at 0.97x for <nick>" when (:slow-for msg) is non-nil, else empty.
  • loops!: status interval 5000 → 2000. Ping stays 5000. The 500 ms policy tick stays.

Risks and open questions

  • A single flaky viewer slows the room. Bounded three ways: the 3 s enter threshold, the 60 s "gone" cut-off, the 15 s staleness cut-off. If it still annoys, the knob is :room-slow-enter. A host-side toggle is deliberately not in this plan; add it only if a real party asks.
  • Audio at 0.95-1.05x. preservesPitch makes it inaudible in Chrome and Firefox; old Safari pitch-shifts slightly. Accepted.
  • Catch-up needs runway, runway needs bandwidth. A viewer who can never buffer 8 s ahead never catches up on their own; the room's 0.97 and a reload are their tools until #7 lowers their rung.
  • Two sources of truth for rate on the client. The element's playbackRate is written only from apply-policy! and the pause handler; nothing else touches it. Keep it that way.
  • Status at 2 s. ~40 bytes per viewer per 2 s; irrelevant.
