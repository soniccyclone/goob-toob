#+OPTIONS: toc:2 num:nil ^:nil

# Status: approved 2026-10-16 (with the give-up rule); implementing on feat/sync-slew.

* Goal
Stop the catch-up spiral for viewers on slow or jittery connections. Today a
viewer 0.3 s behind is pushed to play faster, up to 1.5x, which pulls 1.5x
the bitrate they already could not sustain, so the fix for a stall causes the
next stall. After this plan: the room itself slows to 0.97x while anyone is
meaningfully behind (on by default), a lagging viewer slews back at no more
than 1.05x and only when it has buffer to spare, nobody is ever seeked
forward, and a viewer who wants to jump to the room reloads the page. All
rates stay inside the band humans do not notice.

Decisions already taken on #8: room slow-down on by default; laggards only
slew; reload is the jump; the room gives up on a chronic laggard after a fixed
window (catch-up assumes the link can improve; if it cannot, they stay a
laggard and #7 is their remedy); #7 (sync-aware quality cap, ladder bitrates)
comes after this and is out of scope here.

* Findings
- The server already carries a room rate and never uses it. =room-for= sets
  =:rate 1.0= (~src/clj/goob_toob/services/sync.clj:20~), =state-msg=
  broadcasts it (~sync.clj:24-26~), =expected-pos= honours it
  (~src/cljc/goob_toob/sync_core.cljc:8-14~). Nothing ever writes another
  value.
- The server knows every viewer's lag and stall flag from =status!=
  (~sync.clj:67-71~, fed every 5 s by ~src/cljs/goob_toob/watch.cljs:280-283~)
  and uses it only to paint the peer list.
- The client policy is stateless and absolute: =policy d= (~sync_core.cljc:29-41~)
  has no memory, so a viewer hovering at the 0.3 s edge flips in and out of
  =:catch-up= every 500 ms tick (~watch.cljs:284~); and =:none= forces
  =playbackRate 1.0= (~watch.cljs:63-64~), which would fight any room rate
  other than 1.0 (expected advances at 0.97, the element plays 1.0, the
  viewer drifts ahead 0.03 s/s, nudges to 0.95 after ten seconds, repeat).
- =catch-rate= is =1 + 0.1|d|= capped at 1.5 (~sync_core.cljc:43-48~): 0.3 s
  behind already plays 1.03x forever; 5 s behind plays 1.5x, plainly audible
  and 50 % more bandwidth. =settle-threshold= (~sync_core.cljc:51~) is
  defined and read nowhere.
- Behind by 30 s or more hard-seeks (~sync_core.cljc:39~). For a viewer whose
  problem is bandwidth, a forward seek lands in an empty buffer: a fresh
  stall.
- Peer lag never updates after join. =broadcast-peers= runs only from the
  join and leave paths (~src/clj/goob_toob/ws.clj:60,72~); =beat-msg= carries
  no peers (~sync.clj:28~). The host sees join-time lag until someone leaves.
- Peer lag sign is inverted on display. =status!= stores
  =expected − pos= (positive when behind, ~sync.clj:69~); =ui/fmt-lag=
  prints =behind= for negative values (~src/cljs/goob_toob/ui.cljs:62-66~).
  A viewer 8 s behind shows as "8s ahead".
- Playback rate is the real mechanism, not timestamp jumps. The client sets
  =video.playbackRate= directly (~watch.cljs:65-66~); dash.js's own
  =setPlaybackRate= is the same assignment
  (~node_modules/dashjs/dist/dash.all.debug.js:17506-17508~) and its live
  catch-up feature is built on it. =preservesPitch= is already set at player
  create (~watch.cljs:155-156~).
- Prior art, from the #8 thread: NTP slews under 128 ms offset and steps
  above it (our =:catch-up= / =:seek=); broadcast time compression runs 2-5 %
  fast unnoticed and the tempo JND is about 5 %, so 1.05x is the invisible
  ceiling; Syncplay slows the *ahead* clients to 0.95x once someone is
  >1.5 s behind, which costs the laggard no bandwidth and adds to the closing
  speed. With the room at 0.97 and the laggard at 1.05 the gap closes at
  0.08 s/s: 10 s heals in about two minutes with nobody past 5 %.

* Plan
** 1 · sync_core.cljc: tuning map, stateful policy, room-rate (pure)
Replace =policy=, =catch-rate=, =nudge-slow-rate= and =settle-threshold=
with the following. Everything is data in, data out; no platform calls.

#+begin_src clojure
(def tuning
  {:dead-band       1.0    ; behind by less than this: do nothing
   :settle          0.15   ; hysteresis exit for catch-up and nudge
   :nudge-enter     0.5    ; ahead by more than this: slow to :nudge-rate
   :seek-ahead      1.5    ; ahead by more than this: seek back (buffer has it)
   :catch-rate      1.05   ; absolute playbackRate while catching up
   :nudge-rate      0.95   ; absolute playbackRate while ahead
   :runway          8.0    ; seconds of buffered media required to catch up
   :room-slow-rate  0.97
   :room-slow-enter 3.0    ; someone this far behind: room slows
   :room-slow-exit  1.0    ; everyone within this: room back to 1.0
   :status-fresh-ms 15000  ; older status reports do not count
   :gone-after-s    60.0   ; this far behind is "reload me", not "wait for me"
   :give-up-ms      180000}) ; behind for this long continuously: stop slowing for them

(defn policy
  "prev is the mode from the last tick, d is local - expected in seconds
  (negative = behind), runway is buffered seconds ahead of currentTime.
  Returns :none, :catch-up, :nudge-slow or :seek-back. Never seeks forward."
  [prev d runway]
  (let [{:keys [dead-band settle nudge-enter seek-ahead] runway-min :runway} tuning]
    (cond
      (> d seek-ahead)                                        :seek-back
      (or (> d nudge-enter) (and (= prev :nudge-slow) (> d settle))) :nudge-slow
      (and (>= runway runway-min)
           (or (< d (- dead-band))
               (and (= prev :catch-up) (< d (- settle)))))    :catch-up
      :else                                                   :none)))

(defn rate-for [mode room-rate]
  (case mode
    :catch-up   (:catch-rate tuning)
    :nudge-slow (:nudge-rate tuning)
    room-rate))

(defn room-rate
  "prev is the room's current rate. clients is a seq of
  {:nick s :drift d :age-ms n :behind-ms m} where drift is pos - expected
  (negative = behind), age-ms is how old the report is and behind-ms is how
  long this client has continuously been at or past :room-slow-enter (0 when
  not). Returns {:rate r :for nick} with :for the worst eligible laggard
  while slowed, else nil. A client past :give-up-ms no longer counts."
  [prev clients]
  (let [{:keys [room-slow-rate room-slow-enter room-slow-exit
                status-fresh-ms gone-after-s give-up-ms]} tuning
        eligible (filter #(and (<= (:age-ms %) status-fresh-ms)
                               (<= (- (:drift %)) gone-after-s)
                               (<= (or (:behind-ms %) 0) give-up-ms))
                         clients)
        worst (apply max-key #(- (:drift %)) {:drift 0.0 :nick nil} eligible)
        behind (- (:drift worst))]
    (cond
      (>= behind room-slow-enter) {:rate room-slow-rate :for (:nick worst)}
      (<= behind room-slow-exit)  {:rate 1.0 :for nil}
      :else {:rate prev :for (when (= prev room-slow-rate) (:nick worst))})))

(defn reanchor
  "Change a playing room's rate without moving its position."
  [room now rate]
  (assoc room :pos (expected-pos (assoc room :now now)) :t0 now :rate rate))
#+end_src

Notes for the implementer: =max-key= with the =(- drift)= key picks the
most-behind client; the seed map makes the empty case return =behind = 0=.
Delete =catch-rate=, =nudge-slow-rate= and =settle-threshold= (their values
live in =tuning= now) and fix the two callers in =watch.cljs=. =drift= and
=expected-pos= are unchanged. Keep the ns docstring; add no comments.

** 2 · sync.clj: lag sign, status age, beat-time rate control, peers on the beat
- =status!=: store =:drift (- pos expected)= and =:at (mono-ms)= instead of
  =:lag=. Positive now means ahead, matching =sc/drift=. =peers-list= emits
  =:lag (:drift c)= so the wire name and =ui/fmt-lag= stay as they are and the
  display sign comes out right. Also maintain =:behind-since=: set to =now=
  when it is nil and =(- drift)= is at or past =:room-slow-enter=; set to nil
  when =(- drift)= is at or below =:room-slow-exit=; otherwise leave it.
  =client-drifts= derives =:behind-ms (if behind-since (- now behind-since) 0)=.
  Giving up is therefore per viewer and resets only when they get back
  inside the exit band (a reload also resets it: new cid).
- =host-cmd!=: every op (=play=, =pause=, =seek=) also sets =:rate 1.0=. The
  lag data is stale after a host action; the beat re-evaluates within 2 s.
- New =tick-room!=:
  #+begin_src clojure
  (defn- client-drifts [room now]
    (for [[_ c] (:clients room)]
      {:nick (:nick c) :drift (or (:drift c) 0.0) :age-ms (- now (or (:at c) 0))
       :behind-ms (if-let [t (:behind-since c)] (- now t) 0)}))

  (defn- tick-room! [room-id]
    (let [now (mono-ms)
          room (get @rooms* room-id)
          {:keys [rate for]} (sc/room-rate (:rate room) (client-drifts room now))]
      (when (and (:playing? room) (not= rate (:rate room)))
        (swap! rooms* update room-id sc/reanchor now rate)
        (println "room" room-id "rate" rate (if for (str "for " for) ""))
        (broadcast room-id (assoc (state-msg (get @rooms* room-id)) :op "rate")))
      (let [room (get @rooms* room-id)]
        (broadcast room-id (assoc (beat-msg room)
                                  :slow-for (when (= (:rate room) (:room-slow-rate sc/tuning)) for)
                                  :peers (peers-list room))))))
  #+end_src
  =start-beat-loop!= calls =tick-room!= for each room with clients instead
  of broadcasting =beat-msg= directly. A paused room never changes rate (the
  =playing?= guard) but still beats.
- =broadcast-peers= stays for join/leave (immediate list change).

  (defn- apply-policy! [st video]

            expected (expected-now st room)
            d (sc/drift (.-currentTime video) expected)
            mode (sc/policy (.-mode st) d (buffer-ahead video))

* Risks and open questions
- *A single flaky viewer slows the room.* Bounded four ways: the 3 s enter
  threshold, the 15 s staleness cut-off, the 60 s "gone" cut-off, and the
  3-minute give-up per viewer (decided). At 0.08 s/s closing (room 0.97,
  laggard 1.05) three minutes heals 14 s; a viewer still 3 s or more behind
  after that is not closing and the room stops paying for them. They keep
  the reload hint. The remaining knob is =:room-slow-enter=.
- *The host is a client too.* When the room slows, the host's element runs
  0.97x like everyone else, and every host play/pause/seek snaps the rate to
  1.0 with the beat re-evaluating within 2 s. Expect =server.log= to show
  =rate 1.0= then =rate 0.97= around each host action while a laggard is
  present; that flapping is by design, not a bug.
- *Audio at 0.95-1.05x.* =preservesPitch= makes it inaudible in Chrome and
  Firefox; old Safari pitch-shifts slightly. Accepted.
- *Catch-up needs runway, runway needs bandwidth.* A viewer who can never
  buffer 8 s ahead never catches up on their own; the room's 0.97 (for three
  minutes) and a reload are their tools. By decision no further effort goes
  here: #7 lowers their rung, which is the actual remedy.
- *Two sources of truth for rate on the client.* The element's
  =playbackRate= is written only from =apply-policy!= and the pause handler;
  nothing else touches it. Keep it that way.
- *Status at 2 s.* ~40 bytes per viewer per 2 s; irrelevant.
